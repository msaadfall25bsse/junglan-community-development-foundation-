ABOUT OUR SERVICE

Supporting Access to Essential Medical Transportation

Access to medical facilities can be challenging for families living in remote mountain communities. Long distances, difficult roads, and limited transportation options can make hospital visits difficult. Through its ambulance service, JCDF helps patients travel from their communities to healthcare facilities, providing important transportation support when it is needed.

THE GEOGRAPHICAL CHALLENGE

Reaching Hospitals Is Not Easy for Remote Mountain Communities

Janglan Valley is surrounded by mountainous terrain, where reaching medical facilities can involve long journeys, steep roads, and difficult travel conditions. For residents of remote villages, access to transportation can become a major barrier when they need to visit a hospital.

location_on

Remote Mountain Settlements

Villages are distributed across isolated ridges with zero municipal taxi or transit access.

trending_up

Steep and Unpaved Roads

Loose gravel, severe gradients, and narrow single-track mountain paths restrict ordinary sedans.

timelapse

Long Hospital Travel Distances

Over 35 to 65 kilometers of mountainous transit required to reach district healthcare hubs.

thunderstorm

Seasonal Monsoon and Landslide Conditions

Heavy precipitation frequently triggers rockfall, requiring cautious and experienced mountain driving.

ac_unit

Winter Snow and Difficult Road Access

Sub-zero temperatures and iced mountain passes demand robust mechanical preparation.

Terrain Realities

Overcoming Physical Isolation

Without this dedicated ambulance van, families must rely on open tractors or hazardous pedestrian carriers during acute distress.
